Comparison contract
- No universal item-level assumption or opaque weighted "best" claim. Maintain
  manual job/weapon-skill/role intent; explain exact comparable stat changes.
- Prefer conservative dominance within an established comparable class. Missing
  fields, unparsed/special/conditional/latent effects, augment/extdata differences,
  changed weapon skill or damage/delay/TP tradeoffs remain review/unknown unless
  their semantics are actually modeled. Do not turn a partially parsed item
  description into a guaranteed upgrade.
- Job/level/race/slot and all known equip restrictions first. Preserve weapon
  skill choice; main/offhand, two-hand/hand-to-hand, shield, ranged/ammo coupling,
  paired ear/ring and multi-slot/unique restrictions require whole-plan checks.
- Unknown restrictions or incomplete inventory/status data fail closed for
  automation, while manual equipment controls remain available.
- Deduplicate physical item instances, not merely item IDs. Preserve extdata,
  equipment sets, current selection, bag contents and manual placements.

Command / recovery contract
- Preview is non-mutating. Apply revalidates the snapshot and intent; stale
  plans are invalidated. Only0x050-equivalent equip operations are in scope.
- One outstanding command; compare acknowledgement against requested physical
  item/slot and reject stale/reused inventory indices. Already satisfied is a
  no-op. Repeated Apply cannot become an unequip or duplicate batch.
- A server refusal, missing ack, manual change, job/zone/state change or changed
  inventory stops the remainder and reports confirmed partial results. No
  automatic retry loops or speculative restoration.
- Any Restore Previous action must be separately explicit, revalidated and
  server-acknowledged; never invent items, overwrite saved sets or assume rollback
  succeeded. Item loss is never an acceptable equipment recovery mechanism.
- Never sell/discard/consume/purchase, move bags, or edit character/save state
  to make a recommendation or application possible.
