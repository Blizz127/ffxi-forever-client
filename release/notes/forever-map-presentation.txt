Forever area-map presentation and personal waypoint — owner task ed805cff7911

Scope: Forever flavour on a Forever server. Classic behavior is unchanged.
Based on the preserved v1.0.5 upstream merge (0220cce); no public source push.

Forever Extras now stages its preferences with Apply/OK and Cancel, matching
main Settings. Regular Extras keeps its existing behavior. Under Extras > Maps:
- M Map Placement: Original, Centered (default), Follow Mouse.
- Size: 40–90% of the shorter screen side; default 80%.
- Standing map opacity: 20–100%, default 80%.
- Moving map opacity: 10–100%, default 25%. Markers remain readable.
- Track Player: on by default; updates the live marker and centers the map.
- Waypoint Pins: on by default; can be hidden without losing the session pin.

Follow Mouse places the panel beside the cursor while the character moves,
clamped to the screen. It freezes while stopped or clicking so it is usable.
It never steers the player or moves the system pointer. Player tracking is a
separate option. Left-drag pans and pauses tracking until the existing recenter
key is used or the map is reopened. Wheel over the new canvas zooms. Movement
keys/stick do not also pan the new canvas. Original keeps its prior controls.

M/ESC, map ownership/data, stored zoom, marker files, scans, Change Map, and
HP/quest markers remain. Marker editing/scans/map lists temporarily use the
original canvas. Event/warp/primer maps keep their original controls. The
Ctrl/Alt+O overlay and its saved opacity are separate and untouched.

Right-click an owned area map to set or replace one cyan waypoint. Right-click
the pin or click Clear waypoint to clear it. The pin stores zone, map page and
world position; it survives zoning, not logout/restart. On the matching minimap
page it appears as a marker or edge direction pointer. Other zones/floors do not
show a misleading direction: the Modern minimap names the other zone/page.
There is no auto-travel, routefinding, cross-zone exit guidance or server feed.
The minimap must be enabled; it retains its existing hide-while-main-map-open
behavior. Clear waypoint is available when viewing the pin's map page.
