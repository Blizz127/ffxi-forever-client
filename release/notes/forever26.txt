Forever.26 — circular minimap and waypoint controls

- The Forever Modern minimap is circular, north up and centered on the player.
  Existing zoom, saved placement/size, fold controls and Wide Scan selection
  remain. Map coordinates and the large M map are unchanged.
- Right-click inside the minimap to place or replace the same waypoint used by
  the main map. Clear waypoint removes it from both. Blank corners, missing map
  data, disabled waypoints and Wide Scan range selection cannot place a pin.
- Quest and waypoint edge pointers follow the circular rim. A separate cyan
  diamond on the compass points toward a waypoint in the current zone without
  replacing Wide Scan or call-for-help tracking.
- A different-zone pin remains saved for the session, labeled with its zone,
  but shows no invented cross-zone compass route. Minimap pins are page-specific;
  the compass gives a horizontal same-zone bearing, not floor/path navigation.
- Includes the queued Forever.25 changes: map-settings frame fit, L tracker
  shortcut, active objective defaults, hidden recipient-stamped system feeds,
  next-mission guidance and the optional locally generated Windurst texture pilot.

Forever Modern UI only. Plain minimap geometry, regular FFXI and all saved map
settings remain unchanged. No game data is included. No live .26, owner-device,
native Windows GUI or hardware-performance check is claimed. Existing server,
device, source and actual owner-only delivery holds remain in effect.
