Forever.28 — navigation arrow and mouse-look recovery

Forever Modern UI:
- A movable arrow near the top center points toward the selected tracked
  objective, its next known zone exit, or a personal map waypoint. Horizontal
  distance is shown in FFXI yalms. Gold means objective; cyan means personal pin.
- Auto follows a personal pin first, then the selected objective. Choose
  Objective or Pin explicitly, hide the arrow, or clear a pin using its buttons.
  Settings > Extras > Maps remembers Off/Objective/Waypoint/Auto and 75–150%
  arrow size. Panel position uses the existing saved Modern UI layout.
- Existing saved map settings, map coordinates, markers and panning remain.
  Remote personal pins are retained but have no invented local bearing. Mission
  guidance uses known zone exits. Nearby is a direction hint, not completion,
  pathfinding or a promise of floor/access. No new server feed is needed.

Both classic and Forever:
- Locked and confined mouse-look use raw motion; confined mode no longer warps
  the pointer continuously and feeds synthetic movement back into the camera.
- Failed OS capture falls back to ordinary pointer movement. Map/event takeover
  releases camera capture while retaining map drag coordinates. An old held
  gesture cannot restart camera capture after closing the map.
- Escape cancels a stuck camera gesture before its existing menu duties.
  Focus changes and mod-consumed button releases also release camera ownership.

Owner reports the stuck pointer without opening menus. The map conflict and
confined-motion feedback are concrete source defects, but reproducing the exact
owner-device trigger and native Windows gameplay remains an owner playtest.
The arrow is Forever-only; the generic mouse fixes also ship in the classic drop.
