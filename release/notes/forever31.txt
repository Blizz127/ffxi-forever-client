Forever.31 — recover mouse capture when the map opens

Fixed a native cursor-release failure in the shared input path. Redraw temporarily
holds Gfx outside App; opening a map during camera drag could clear the old capture
flag while skipping the OS ungrab because App.gfx was absent. Later recovery saw
no flag, leaving the pointer confined and camera motion stopped.

Camera capture now retains the actual native window until successful release,
independent of redraw ownership. A failed native release keeps its handle for
retry and suppresses further camera motion. Escape and focus changes can also
release an orphaned native grab even without old capture bookkeeping. A gesture
interrupted by a map stays blocked until its buttons are released; a fresh press
then starts camera movement normally. Map panning, M typing/binding suppression,
waypoints and user settings are preserved.

Shared fix for classic and Forever. The .30 layout/50%-arrow defaults remain in
Forever. No owner-device input, forced restart, account/login or server change.
Local checks do not establish the exact owner-device trigger or native Windows
playtest; those remain owner verification after the release is available.
