Forever.35 — middle-click autorun (Forever only)

A fresh middle press in the focused, unobstructed game view toggles ordinary
autorun. Starting follows the camera's horizontal facing through normal movement
and collision. Press again to stop. Holding/repeated press events and release do
not toggle again. A click which cancels Auto Navigation is stop-only; release and
make a new deliberate click before starting ordinary autorun.

Middle no longer also grabs/orbits the camera in Forever. Right-button camera
behavior and wheel zoom remain. Do not combine the autorun click with another held
mouse button, modifiers or an existing camera grab. UI-consumed clicks, HUD/menu/
map/text input, inactive focus, loading/zoning, unsafe/unavailable movement states
and other scripted movement cannot start autorun. A valid stop click still works
when movement readiness changes after starting. Existing Records of Eminence
middle-page action and mod-consumed input retain priority. Keyboard/controller
saved assignments are untouched; this writes no preference or layout fields.
Regular/classic retains its existing middle-camera behavior and stays vana.15.

Focused sequence/gating/camera-capture checks, both flavor compile checks,
Forever Linux/Windows builds, offline Linux startup and Windows Wine CLI evidence
are separate. No real account, live movement, owner-device input/restart or native
Windows gameplay check. Existing sole publisher retains baton, .34 rollback.
No server, source-push, access-policy or game-data distribution change.
