Forever.36 — stop misleading quest guidance through walls (Forever only)

The selected quest/mission arrow now needs a clear local approach in the loaded
collision model. Before showing it, the client checks the whole approach, up to
64 yalms, for floor continuity, walls/closed doors and zone boundaries. It stops
short for manual interaction. Longer or blocked/unknown approaches show an
explicit manual-navigation reason without a directional arrow. Objective map
numbers remain location markers, not routes. Personal pins remain bearings only.

Auto Navigation also rechecks the complete approach immediately before starting;
its existing live short-corridor, manual-input and timeout stops remain. The
checks never open doors, cross zone triggers, move the physics body during probing,
start movement automatically, or write player settings/layouts. Geometry probes
are bounded and refreshed at most four times per second. They are local collision
checks, not a full pathfinder or proof of server-side door/access permission.
