Forever.38 — original environmental texture preview (Forever only)

Settings → Graphics → Environment Textures → Tower Preview adds new original
stone slabs and masonry to selected Heavens Tower surfaces. Choose Forever
Graphics → Enhanced as well, then Apply. The textures load when the zone next
loads; re-enter the zone or relog voluntarily. Original restores installed art
at the next zone load. No automatic restart, zone change or preference migration.
The new choice defaults off and preserves existing saved graphics/customization.

Two original text-generated material images replace 91 audited opaque bindings
in the installed test fixture: 68 floor bindings and 23 masonry bindings. Source
floor 128x256 becomes 887x1774; masonry 128x128 becomes 1254x1254, with mipmaps.
The images were generated from text alone. No game textures were submitted to
image generation and no source-derived DAT/texture pack is distributed.

Exact stock-content matches only. Altered/custom textures, transparent uses,
animated meshes, decorative atlases and other zones retain their installed art.
Geometry, texture coordinates, collision, doors and original bump-map suffixes
are preserved. The existing Enhanced lighting/effects are unchanged. This is a
limited material preview, not a whole-zone/world remaster or a general UV fix.
Other tower material bands and borders remain stock and visibly differ in detail.
The initial irregular paving study is not included in the compiled preview.

Focused settings Apply/Cancel/persistence/flavour checks, explicit owned-DAT
binding/geometry checks, offline before/after floor/wall scenes and Original-mode
render invariance are recorded with the release, followed by distribution builds
and exact-binary local startup/render checks. In the development software renderer,
zone texture allocation including mipmaps increased from1.5MB to18.3MB. This is not
an owner-GPU performance measurement. No real account, live gameplay, owner-device
input/restart or native Windows GUI test is claimed.

Regular classic remains vana.15; enhanced environment code/assets are compiled
out of classic. Existing sole launcher publisher, owner-native audience and .37
rollback. No server restart/deploy, source push or access-policy change. Existing
held door, Home Point/Survival, 0x114, source and device checks remain held.
