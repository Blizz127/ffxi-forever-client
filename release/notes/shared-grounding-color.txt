Shared grounding and scene colour — Vana.12 / Forever.23

Both flavours use the same contact-shadow footprint on collision geometry and
on the fallback flat quad. Previously the projected blob had half the diameter;
small actors could lose most of their visible grounding, and switching shadow
paths changed the footprint. This is a shared quality adjustment to the original
blob, not a new lighting style. Strength, height fade, depth testing, receiver
geometry and the user's Shadows Off choice are unchanged.

Character, scenery and sky shaders now use the exact piecewise sRGB transfer
at texture/framebuffer boundaries, including clear colour. Hardware sRGB texture
decoding was previously undone with a gamma-2.2 approximation, changing authored
dark colours before the lighting calculation. Legacy D3D8 lighting still runs
in its authored encoded colour space. Actor-specific weather/area light values
remain intact; no global character-brightness reduction or classic filmic grade.
Transfer reference: https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#color-conversion-code

The earlier CPU actor lighting-metadata fix, comfort/frame pacing defaults,
16x filtering/mipmaps and optional edge AA remain. Enhanced sun shadows, lamp
lighting, AO, bloom, tone grade, atmosphere and water remain Forever-only and
toggleable. Locally generated HD textures are a later pilot; no game data is
included in either drop. No replacement engine.

Focused validation: a GPU check compares all 256 grey levels with actual sRGB
texture decoding; offline character-select renders cover a small daytime actor
and a full-height night actor. Release records contain actual build/check results.
Offline generic models do not reproduce the owner's exact equipment or device.
Software rendering is not a frame-pacing or comfort measurement. Owner playtest,
native Windows GUI and live gameplay remain separate checks.

Owner playtest: compare feet/ground contact at character select and in a zone;
check dark clothing, scenery and sky together at day/night; try Shadows Off and
normal shadow settings. Check comfort at the retained 60 or 30 FPS cap. Report
scene, settings and screenshot for any new colour or shadow artifacts.

Regular publication retains the publisher's zero-FFXI-human gate and rollback.
Door live retest, HP/Survival expansion-mask and destination 0x114 checks, and
public-source publication remain held. This increment does not resume them.
